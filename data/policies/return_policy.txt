# Return Policy

## 1. Overview

Customers may request a return for eligible products within 30 calendar days from the date the product was delivered.

All returns are subject to the eligibility conditions described in this policy.

## 2. Return Eligibility

A product is eligible for return when:

* The return request is submitted within 30 calendar days of delivery.
* The product is unused and in resalable condition.
* The original packaging, accessories, manuals, and included items are returned where applicable.
* The product does not show damage caused by misuse, accidents, modification, or improper handling.
* The product is not listed under the non-returnable products section.

## 3. Non-Returnable Products

The following products may not be eligible for return:

* Personalized or customized products.
* Products that have been used or damaged after delivery.
* Products with missing essential accessories or components.
* Items specifically marked as non-returnable at the time of purchase.
* Products restricted from return because of hygiene or safety requirements.

## 4. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If a customer receives a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, the customer should contact support as soon as possible.

The customer may be asked to provide:

* Order ID.
* Description of the problem.
* Photographs or videos showing the damage or incorrect item.
* Packaging photographs when relevant.

Support may review the submitted information before approving the return or replacement.

## 5. Return Request Process

Customers can request a return by providing their order ID and the reason for the return.

The support system should verify:

1. Whether the order exists.
2. Whether the product is eligible for return.
3. Whether the request is within the 30-day return period.
4. Whether any special restrictions apply.

If eligible, the customer will receive instructions for completing the return.

## 6. Return Shipping

Return shipping responsibility depends on the reason for the return.

For products that arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect, the company may provide return-shipping assistance according to the applicable case.

For customer-initiated returns such as changing one's mind, return shipping may be the customer's responsibility.

## 7. Inspection

Returned products may be inspected after they are received.

If the returned product does not satisfy the eligibility requirements, the company may reject the return or adjust the available resolution.

## 8. Exchanges

Where available, eligible products may be exchanged instead of refunded.

Exchange availability depends on product availability and the specific circumstances of the return.

## 9. Policy Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy are not automatic.

Customers requiring an exception should contact customer support. Support may review the case based on the order history and circumstances.

## 10. Important Rule

The 30-day return period begins from the date the product is delivered, not from the date the customer submits the order.

Final return eligibility is determined after reviewing the order and applicable product conditions.
