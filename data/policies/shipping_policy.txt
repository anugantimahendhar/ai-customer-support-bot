# Shipping Policy

## 1. Overview

Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation.

Standard shipping generally takes 5 to 7 business days after the order has been shipped.

Estimated delivery dates are provided as estimates and may change because of carrier delays, weather, holidays, incorrect addresses, or other unforeseen circumstances.

## 2. Order Processing

Orders normally enter processing after payment is confirmed.

Processing time may vary depending on:

* Product availability.
* Order volume.
* Payment verification.
* Customized or special-order products.
* Business holidays.

An order that is still processing may not yet have a tracking number.

## 3. Shipping Statuses

The system may display the following statuses:

### Processing

The order has been received but has not yet been shipped.

### Confirmed

The order has been accepted and is being prepared for fulfillment.

### Shipped

The package has left the fulfillment facility and has been handed to the shipping carrier.

### Out for Delivery

The carrier indicates that the package is scheduled for delivery.

### Delivered

The carrier indicates that the package has been delivered.

### Delayed

The shipment has experienced a carrier or processing delay.

### Cancelled

The order has been cancelled and will not continue through normal fulfillment.

## 4. Order Tracking

Customers can request the current status of an order by providing the order ID.

When available, support may provide:

* Current order status.
* Tracking number.
* Estimated delivery date.
* Available shipment information.

The estimated delivery date is not a guaranteed delivery date.

## 5. Delivery Delays

Delivery may be delayed because of:

* Severe weather.
* Public holidays.
* Carrier operational issues.
* Incorrect or incomplete delivery addresses.
* High shipping volumes.
* Customs or regulatory processing.
* Unexpected transportation disruptions.

Customers should allow additional time when a carrier delay is reported.

## 6. Incorrect Delivery Address

Customers should verify their shipping address before placing an order.

If an order is still in processing, support may be able to assist with an address correction.

Once an order has been shipped, changing the delivery address may not be possible.

## 7. Lost or Missing Packages

If tracking information shows that a package was delivered but the customer cannot locate it, the customer should first check:

* Delivery location.
* Mailroom or reception area.
* Household members.
* Neighbors where appropriate.

If the package remains missing, the customer should contact support for further investigation.

## 8. International Shipping

International shipments may require additional processing time because of customs, import requirements, or local carrier procedures.

Customers may be responsible for applicable duties, taxes, or customs charges where required.

## 9. Estimated Delivery

Estimated delivery information is based on available shipping and carrier data.

The system should not represent an estimated delivery date as a guaranteed delivery date.

For real-time order questions, the order database should be checked before providing the current status.

## 10. Important Rule

The AI assistant must distinguish between an estimated delivery date and a confirmed delivery event.

For example:

* "Estimated delivery: August 14" means the package is expected around that date.
* "Delivered" means the carrier has reported the package as delivered.
