# Refund Policy

## 1. Overview

Refunds are available for eligible returned products after the return has been received and reviewed.

A refund is not automatically guaranteed simply because a return request has been submitted.

## 2. Refund Eligibility

A customer may qualify for a refund when:

* The order is eligible for return.
* The return request was submitted within the applicable return period.
* The returned product satisfies the return conditions.
* The returned product has been received and successfully reviewed.

## 3. Refund Process

The general refund process is:

1. Customer submits a return request.
2. Support verifies return eligibility.
3. Return instructions are provided when applicable.
4. Customer returns the product.
5. The returned product is received.
6. The product may be inspected.
7. The refund is approved or rejected.
8. An approved refund is sent to the original payment method.

## 4. Refund Method

Approved refunds are normally issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.

The company may not be able to redirect a refund to a different payment method.

## 5. Refund Timing

The time required for a refund depends on the refund-processing process and the customer's bank or payment provider.

A customer should not be told that a refund will appear immediately after approval.

The assistant should distinguish between:

* Refund approved by the company.
* Refund processed by the payment system.
* Refund received by the customer's bank account or card.

## 6. Partial Refunds

A partial refund may apply when only part of an order is eligible for a refund or when the applicable resolution does not cover the full purchase amount.

The exact refund amount depends on the order and the applicable policy.

## 7. Shipping Charges

Whether original or return shipping charges are refundable depends on the reason for the return and the applicable return conditions.

For damaged, defective, or incorrect products, support may review whether shipping costs qualify for reimbursement.

## 8. Refund Rejection

A refund may be rejected when:

* The product is outside the applicable return period.
* The product does not satisfy return conditions.
* The product is non-returnable.
* Required components or accessories are missing.
* The product shows damage caused after delivery by misuse or improper handling.

Customers may contact support if they believe a refund decision was incorrect.

## 9. Cancelled Orders

If an order is successfully cancelled before shipment, the refund process may be initiated according to the payment and order status.

An order that has already been shipped may not be eligible for normal cancellation and may instead require the applicable return process.

## 10. Refund Status Questions

When a customer asks about a specific refund, the assistant should first collect the order ID.

The assistant should not claim that a refund has been approved or processed unless the relevant system provides that information.

## 11. Important Rule

The AI assistant must not invent refund information.

If the database does not contain refund status information, the assistant should explain that it cannot confirm the refund status and should direct the customer to support for verification.
